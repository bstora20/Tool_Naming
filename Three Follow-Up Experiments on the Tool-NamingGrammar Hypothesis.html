Three Follow-Up Experiments on the Tool-Naming/Grammar Hypothesis

Project: tool_name

This report covers three follow-up experiments requested after the Condition D ("near-miss grammar") study, which had rejected the user's original hypothesis that familiar names + wrong documented grammar is worse than unfamiliar names + correct grammar. The three follow-ups probe whether that rejection was an artifact of (1) find/grep's weak convention, (2) a single mild rule-change, or (3) the shell interface specifically.
